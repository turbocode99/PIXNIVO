How to Create Clean White Background Product Photos for E-Commerce

14 June 2026•7 min read

Major e-commerce marketplaces (Amazon, Google Shopping, eBay, Shopify) require main hero product images to have pure, seamless RGB #FFFFFF white backgrounds.

Shooting on a real white sheet usually yields a muddy grey cast due to shadows and uneven exposure. Here is how to achieve pristine marketplace-compliant catalog photos without studio gear.

Marketplace Standard Specifications

PlatformBackground StandardMinimum Resolution
AmazonPure RGB (255, 255, 255)1000 × 1000 px (Zoom trigger)
Google ShoppingWhite or light neutral800 × 800 px
Shopify StoreConsistent white / transparent2048 × 2048 px recommended
eBayPure white / off-white1600 px longest side

The Three-Step Catalog Workflow

  1. Capture with strong contrast: Place the merchandise against any uncluttered contrasting backdrop so the neural segmenter can clearly identify edges.
  2. One-Click AI Background Removal: Drop the image into PIXNIVO's neural studio to isolate the item instantly.
  3. Add Pure White Canvas & Shadow: Switch the background fill to pure #FFFFFF or export as a transparent PNG.

Pro Conversion Tip: Products with clean white backgrounds convert 27% higher on average because listings look professional, uniform, and trustworthy.
