Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: Most PIXNIVO tools process your files entirely in your browser, so they never leave your device. Two optional features — the Enhanced engine in AI OCR and Word/Excel barcode export — send data to our server for processing. Those files are handled in memory, are not stored, and are deleted as soon as the result is returned.

1. Files processed on your device

When you compress, edit, upscale or remove the background from an image, create, merge or compress a PDF, design a logo, generate barcodes or export them as PDF/PNG, or use the Private engine in AI OCR, your files are read and processed by your web browser. They are not transmitted to our servers or to any third party.

2. Features that use our server

Some features need server-side processing. When you use them, the following data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • AI OCR — Enhanced engine: the image or PDF you choose is uploaded so our server can recognize the text. It is processed in temporary memory, is not saved, and is deleted once the extracted text is sent back to you. You can use the Private engine instead to keep the file on your device.
  • Barcode export to Word or Excel: the barcode values and the barcode images generated in your browser are sent so the server can assemble the document. They are not saved. PDF and PNG exports are created on your device.

We do not sell your files or data, do not keep copies of files you process, and do not use them to train AI models.

3. AI models

Background removal and the Private OCR engine download AI model files to your browser the first time you use them. These downloads come from public model and code hosting services (such as Hugging Face and jsDelivr), which can see standard request information like your IP address. Your own images are processed locally after the model has loaded.

4. Analytics & advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregated site traffic (for example page views, browser types and countries), and Google AdSense to show advertising that keeps the tools free. These services may set cookies and process data as described in Google's privacy policy.

5. Data stored in your browser

PIXNIVO stores a few preferences in your browser's local storage, such as your light/dark theme choice and your preferred OCR engine. AI model files may be cached by your browser to make later visits faster. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at support@pixnivo.app.
