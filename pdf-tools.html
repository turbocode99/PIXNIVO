    Privacy-First Architecture & FAQ

    Built with free and open-source software libraries. Client-side tools execute directly in your browser, keeping documents completely private on your device.

    Are documents uploaded to external servers?

    No. Client-side operations (Merge, Split, Rotate, Crop, Organize, Protect, Unlock, Sign, Edit, Watermark, Page Numbers, Forms, Compare, PDF to JPG) run 100% locally in your browser memory. Conversion operations (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Redact, Repair, Summarize) are processed securely through our dedicated, open-source backend core without data retention.

    Is there any file size limit or cost?

    There are no subscriptions or paywalls. All 32 tools are free to use. Processing speed depends on your device's memory for client operations, making standard documents instant on both phones and desktops.

    Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?

    Yes. Pixnivo is fully responsive across iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. You can easily pick documents from your mobile Files or Photos app.
