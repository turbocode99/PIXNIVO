Photos Too Large for Email: Why It Happens & How to Fix It in Seconds

7 June 2026•6 min read

You try to email five photos to a colleague or client, and your email client immediately returns: "Message blocked: Attachment size exceeds the 25 MB server limit."

Modern smartphone cameras (48 MP to 200 MP sensors) generate 6 MB to 18 MB per single exposure. Attaching three raw photos instantly breaches mail transfer protocols.

Mail Provider Hard Attachment Limits

The Base64 Overhead Trap: Email protocols encode binary attachments into ASCII text, inflating raw file sizes by approximately 33%. A 20 MB file behaves like a 26.6 MB payload to mail servers.
