About us

About PIXNIVO

PIXNIVO is a free collection of image, PDF and document tools built around one idea: everyday file tasks shouldn't require handing your files to someone else.

Why we built it

A job portal wants a photo under 100 KB. A messaging app turns a family picture into a blurry mess. A marketplace insists on a clean white product background. These are small, everyday problems — yet most online tools solve them by asking you to upload your photo or document to a server you know nothing about.

For an ID photo, a signed contract or a private picture, that's an unnecessary risk. So we built tools that do the work on your own device wherever that's possible, and tell you clearly when it isn't.

What runs on your device

These tools process your files entirely inside your web browser. Your files are never uploaded:

  • Image tools — compress, edit, upscale and remove backgrounds. Background removal uses an AI model that your browser downloads once and then runs locally.
  • PDF tools — turn images into PDFs, merge files and compress PDFs.
  • Logo Generator — design, preview and export your logo.
  • Barcode Generator — create barcodes and export them as PDF or PNG.
  • AI OCR, Private engine — text recognition that runs in your browser.

When our server is involved

A few features need more computing power or document formats that browsers can't produce on their own. They are clearly labeled, and you can always choose an on-device alternative:

  • AI OCR, Enhanced engine — your file is sent over an encrypted connection, processed in memory and deleted as soon as the text is returned.
  • Barcode export to Word or Excel — the barcode values and images are sent to build the document and are not stored.

We don't keep copies of your files, and we never use them to train AI models.

Our commitments

  • No account needed — no sign-up, email or password.
  • Free, with no watermarks — the site is supported by advertising, not by locking features.
  • Clear about data — every tool tells you whether it runs on your device or on our server.
  • Honest results — our guides explain what each tool can and can't do, so you know what to expect.

Good to know

  • The first background removal downloads the AI model. Your browser keeps it, so later photos start much faster.
  • Speed depends on your device — a recent laptop takes a few seconds, while older phones can take noticeably longer.
  • Busy images where the subject and background share similar colors may need a few strokes with the touch-up eraser.
