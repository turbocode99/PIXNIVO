Which barcode should I use?

Pick the right symbology

QR Code

Links, Wi-Fi details, contact cards and any text. Scannable by every phone camera.

Code 128

Internal labels, shipping and asset tags. Compact and supports letters, numbers and symbols.

EAN-13 / UPC-A

Retail products sold in stores. Requires a registered GS1 company prefix for real products.

GS1-128 / DataMatrix

Supply chain and healthcare data such as GTIN, batch and expiry, written as (01)…(17)…(10)….
