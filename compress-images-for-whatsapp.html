How to Compress Images for WhatsApp Without Losing Quality

12 July 2026•6 min read

You take a sharp picture, send it on WhatsApp, and the recipient says it looks blurry. The original is razor sharp in your phone gallery. So what happened in between?

WhatsApp compressed it aggressively to conserve its server bandwidth. Once you understand the algorithm, you can preserve quality easily.

What WhatsApp does to your photos

  • Resizes dimensions: Downscales the longest side to ~1,600 pixels (throwing away 60% of sensor detail).
  • Re-encodes with heavy lossy JPEG quantization: Introduces visible artifact ringing around text and sharp edges.
  • Strips EXIF metadata: Discards camera parameters and orientation tags.

Generational loss: When someone forwards a received WhatsApp photo, the compression pass repeats. After 3 forwards, text becomes unreadable.

Strategy 1: Send as an Uncompressed Document

  1. In the chat window, tap the + or paperclip icon.
  2. Choose Document instead of Gallery/Photos.
  3. Browse and attach the original image file.

WhatsApp delivers documents byte-for-byte with zero compression.
