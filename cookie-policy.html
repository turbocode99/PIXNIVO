Privacy & Compliance

Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Compliant with GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, CCPA/CPRA, and Google Publisher Policies

Summary: PIXNIVO uses cookies and local browser storage to keep our client-side processing tools functional, remember your appearance settings (such as dark mode), collect aggregated usage metrics via Google Analytics 4, and display non-intrusive advertisements through Google AdSense. You can adjust your cookie settings at any time using the control button below.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

A cookie is a small piece of data (text file) that a website asks your browser to store on your device when you visit in order to remember information about you, such as your language preferences, theme choice, or session state. Similar technologies include localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB, which store client-side state directly within your modern browser without transmitting it across the network on every HTTP request.

Pixnivo is engineered on an in-browser processing architecture (WebAssembly, Web Workers, Canvas, PDF.js, and ONNX Runtime). Your uploaded documents, images, and barcodes remain completely private on your local hardware. Cookies are strictly utilized to ensure technical operation, analyze aggregate traffic, and serve advertisements that fund our free platform.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify the cookies and browser storage keys used on pixnivo.app into three distinct tiers:

Tier A: Strictly Necessary (Essential)

These identifiers are required for core technical functionality, security, and user accessibility. They cannot be deactivated without degrading essential site features.

  • pixnivo_theme (localStorage): Stores your dark/light UI mode selection. Persists indefinitely until cleared.
  • pixnivo_consent_v2 (localStorage): Records your cookie preferences and Google Consent Mode v2 choices so you are not repeatedly prompted. Persists for 12 months.
  • pixnivo_aidc_history (localStorage): Stores your local generated barcode history and favorites directly on your device. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • Security tokens and load-balancing cookies: Issued by Azure Infrastructure and Nginx to maintain active HTTPS sessions and prevent Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.

Tier B: Performance and Analytics

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-9RDW8F1H9D) to measure how visitors interact with Pixnivo. These cookies collect aggregated, anonymized telemetry such as page navigation paths, session duration, device operating systems, screen resolutions, and country-level geographic location.

  • _ga (HTTP Cookie): Used to distinguish unique anonymous users across browsing sessions. Lifespan: 2 years.
  • _ga_9RDW8F1H9D (HTTP Cookie): Used to persist session state across page reloads. Lifespan: 2 years.

All analytics data is processed with IP anonymization enabled by default, ensuring your complete IP address is never stored or logged by Google Analytics.

Tier C: Advertising and Marketing (Google AdSense)

To provide all 32+ PDF tools, image compressors, background removers, OCR, and AIDC barcode suites 100% free of charge without requiring subscriptions or paywalls, Pixnivo partners with Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-8972850134795669) and Google-certified ad networks.

  • __gads, __gpi: Set by Google AdSense to measure ad impressions, frequency capping (preventing you from seeing the same ad repeatedly), and fraud prevention. Lifespan: 13 months.
  • IDE, DSID, NID: Set by Google's DoubleClick ad exchange on the domain doubleclick.net to serve relevant, contextual, or personalized advertisements based on user interests. Lifespan: up to 12 months.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie Disclosure

Under Google AdSense Program Policies, we explicitly disclose the following mechanisms:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to Pixnivo or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its certified partners to serve targeted advertisements to users based on their browsing patterns across the web.
  • Users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland are provided with a Google Consent Mode v2 consent prompt upon their initial visit. When non-essential cookies are declined, Google AdSense automatically switches to serving non-personalized contextual ads, and advertising cookies are withheld.

4. How to Control and Opt Out of Advertising Cookies

You have full legal control over how your data is utilized for digital advertising. You can exercise your choices through any of the following independent avenues:

  • Pixnivo Preferences Center: Click Cookie Settings at any time to disable Analytics or Advertising cookies directly on this site.
  • Google Ads Settings: Manage or turn off personalized advertising across Google services at https://adssettings.google.com/.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): Opt out of interest-based ads from hundreds of participating ad networks at https://optout.aboutads.info/.
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): For European users, manage ad preferences at https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): Opt out at https://optout.networkadvertising.org/.

5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage, inspect, block, or delete cookies through their internal settings. Please refer to your browser's official support documentation:

  • Google Chrome Cookie Management
  • Apple Safari Cookie Settings
  • Mozilla Firefox Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Microsoft Edge Privacy & Cookies

Please note: Blocking all cookies may cause certain visual customizations (such as your dark mode preference) to reset on subsequent visits.

6. Regulatory Compliance Statements

European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR & ePrivacy Directive)

In adherence to EU Article 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy Directive, non-essential cookies (Analytics and Advertising) are not placed on your device until affirmative consent has been granted through our Google Consent Mode v2 compliant banner. You can revoke or alter this consent at any moment.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Pixnivo does not sell personal user information. However, you may disable targeted advertising cookies anytime via our Cookie Settings or by transmitting the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.
